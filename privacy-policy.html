Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Digi Vidyarthi ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website digividyarthi.com — Varanasi's leading digital marketing institute. This page explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and what your rights are.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact form data: name, phone number, email address, course interest, and any message you send through our contact form.
  • Analytics data: anonymized page views, device type, and country via Microsoft Clarity.
  • Cookies: functional cookies to remember UI preferences; no advertising cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries about courses, free demo classes, and admissions.
  • To send you information about upcoming batches if you have opted in.
  • To improve our website and courses through aggregated analytics.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Hostinger (hosting) — for serving our website.
  • Microsoft Clarity (analytics) — under their own privacy terms.
  • Government authorities — when legally required.

4. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are kept for up to 12 months for follow-ups, then deleted. Analytics data is retained per Microsoft's default retention policy.

5. Your Rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023 and GDPR (where applicable), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any right, email Info@digividyarthi.com with subject "Privacy Request".

6. Data Security

We use HTTPS, secure password hashing (bcrypt via PHP password_hash), environment-based credential storage, honeypot anti-spam, and HTTP security headers (CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options) to keep your data safe.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
